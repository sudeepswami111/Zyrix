How this classifier works (under the hood)

1 Dataset — 48 labelled reviews trained the model (train_sentiment.py).
2 TF-IDF Vectorizer — converts each sentence into a vector of numbers (one per vocabulary word), because models cannot read raw text.
3 Logistic Regression — learned weights for each word: words like "amazing" get high positive weights; "terrible" gets high negative weights.
4 Same vectorizer at inference — your typed text is converted using the SAVED vocabulary from training. A new vectorizer would break predictions.
5 Confidence — the model's output is a probability (0-100%). Higher = more certain. Below 60% means the sentence is ambiguous to the model.
