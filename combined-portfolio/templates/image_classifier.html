CNN Architecture used for this classifier

Input
32x32x3 → Conv2D

16x16 → Conv2D
64 filters → MaxPool
8x8 → Flatten
4096 vals → Dense
64 neurons → Output
Sigmoid

Conv2D slides small 3x3 filters across the image, learning to detect edges and shapes. MaxPooling halves the spatial size, reducing params and preventing overfitting. The final Sigmoid activation maps the outputs to a probability range [0, 1].
